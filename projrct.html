indian flag


Our tricolor flag is the living heartbeat of our nation, weaving courage, peace, and growth into every fold.

The National Flag of India, affectionately known as the Tiranga (meaning "tricolor"), is a horizontal rectangular emblem of the nation's sovereignty, unity, and heritage. Designed by freedom fighter Pingali Venkayya and officially adopted on July 22, 1947, the flag consists of three equal bands: saffron at the top representing courage and sacrifice, white in the middle symbolizing peace and truth, and green at the bottom signifying faith and chivalry. At the center of the white band sits the Ashoka Chakra, a navy-blue wheel with 24 spokes derived from the Lion Capital of Ashoka at Sarnath, which represents the eternal wheel of law, motion, and continuous progress. Preserved under strict guidelines outlined in the Flag Code of India, the tricolor serves as a profound symbol of national pride, honored across the country on occasions like Independence Day and Republic Day.
